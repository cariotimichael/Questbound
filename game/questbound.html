How to Play

Goal

Aid the town of Emberhold: complete every quest, reach level 10, slay Cragmaw (level 8) in the Emberdark Cave north-east — and then descend through the Ruined Spire gate south of town into the Emberdeep to face Kezath, the Sealed Flame (level 10). Speak to townsfolk marked with ! to pick up quests, and return to them when you see ?.

Talents

Every level grants a talent point. Open Talents from the side buttons or the T key to rank up talents that change how your abilities feel — cheaper, bigger, chained, or entirely new side effects. Reshuffle freely: removing or resetting is free.

Options

Open Options from the title screen, the ⚙ side button, or the O key: master / music / sound-effects volume with independent mute switches, particle effects, screen shake, damage numbers, vignette, the quest tracker, and a fantasy-themed mouse cursor (which changes over enemies, allies and interactables) can all be tuned or hidden here. Every hotkey for abilities, movement and interacting can be rebound to your liking. Settings are saved on this device.

Races & Racial Passives

• Emberkin — Inner Fire: +8% to all damage.
• Ogrun — Thick Hide: take 10% less damage from all sources, +5% max health.
• Astren — Arcane Mind: +15% mana regeneration, abilities cost 8% less mana.
• Wildkin — Keen Eye: +10% critical strike chance.
• Human — Adaptability: +10% XP from all sources.
Racials benefit every class — pick the race that fits how you want to play.

Necromancer

The Necromancer wears Cloth and wields Staves. Abilities: Raise Skeleton (Lv1) — a bone servant that fights for you; Life Drain (Lv3) — damage a foe and heal yourself; Plague Nova (Lv5) — pestilent AoE that spreads death around you; Soul Harvest (Lv7) — reap the life of every foe around you and turn their wounds into your strength. Plagued enemies take damage over time.

Class Quests

Every class has a mentor with a 6-quest chain — a trial roughly per level, ending with Cragmaw — each rewarding a piece of class-locked gear suited to that level. Seek out Veteran Korr (Warrior), Archmage Lyra (Mage), Tracker Fen (Hunter), or Mortis at the graveyard (Necromancer).

Specialized Vendors

Weaponsmith Wex sells weapons, Armorer Brida sells head/chest/leg/hand/foot armor, and Trader Pell sells consumables and trinkets — always matching your class. Vendor gear, world drops, and class-quest rewards are all competitive: gear however you like.

Gear & Classes

Monsters drop green, blue, epic and legendary gear for 7 slots — weapon, head, chest, legs, hands, feet, trinket. Tap a piece in your bags to equip it; equipped gear shows on your sprite. Each class is locked to its own armor and weapons: Warriors wear Plate and swing Greatswords, Mages wear Cloth and carry Staves, Hunters wear Leather and wield Bows, Necromancers wear Cloth and wield Bone Staves.

Appearance

Pick your character's race, skin tone, hair color, hairstyle, eye color and a racial accessory on the creation screen — the preview window shows your hero as you'll play them. Each race's palettes stay true to its theme.

Tips

• Monsters leash back home if you run far — use it to escape.
• Stock up on potions before the gnoll camp and the cave.
• Your progress saves automatically; Continue from the title screen.
• New ability slot: your fourth class ability unlocks at level 7 and sits in slot 4 (press 4), with potions moving to slot 5.
